# Rights and provenance

The authoritative license scope is [LICENSE](../LICENSE): project-authored software is GPL-3.0-or-later; project-owned artwork, scene assets and documentation are CC-BY-SA-4.0. Both allow commercial use under their respective terms.

[Release provenance](release/PROVENANCE.md) identifies the retained art and render dependencies, including generated character artwork and the original Colosseum direction. Blender and separately licensed third-party materials retain their own terms.

Purchased game data, third-party reference collections, private source photographs, correspondence and commercial music are excluded from the public release. Earlier exploratory rights inquiries did not grant third-party permissions and are not required to render the original released scene.

Historical study assets require a separate ownership and privacy review before publication; the project's license does not automatically cover everything in the local workspace. See the [archive assessment](release/ARCHIVING.md).
