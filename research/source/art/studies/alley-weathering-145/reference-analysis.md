## Highest-value visual hierarchy

The broad warm/cool organization should remain legible before any pits, bolts or marks are added. Primary loss should read next through missing material and thickness. Pits, hole and crack should be distinct from one another at native delivery scale. Bolts and fine ink should enrich the plane without becoming its dominant pattern. A proof that contains all seven named items can still fail if their scale, causality or density is wrong.

Preserve component boundaries: coating loss is not automatically wall-depth destruction, a pit is not a through-hole, and a warm highlight is not automatically rust. Rust should be darker and more muted than the dominant sunlit tan. Keep original clean component geometry and optional unique damage/weathering as separate editable layers.

## Review views and lighting proxy

Use the same panel dimensions, camera, placement and render settings for before/after. Deliver (1) native4K camera crop without enlargement, (2) the full production camera frame resized to the usual approximately1800px display width and a crop cut from that image, (3) a neutral/clay geometry view, (4) grayscale beauty, and (5) a simple relighting contact sheet. An isolated hero closeup is useful for construction but cannot substitute for the actual camera placement.

For the relighting proxy, freeze materials, texture coordinates, exposure and camera. Render the accepted production key first; then change key azimuth approximately+45degrees and-45degrees at fixed elevation and energy; finally use a low neutral diffuse fill or direct-key-off view. These are diagnostic renders only, not changes to the locked scene lighting. If useful, include one slightly rotated duplicate panel to expose orientation dependence.

Expected response: the broad illumination-driven warm region and small rim catches respond coherently to key direction and occlusion; fixed coating losses, rust origins and material boundaries stay attached to the same surface coordinates. Cool shade remains plausible, and cavities retain depth. Avoid a camera-facing mask that stays lit when the key turns away, or a procedural mask that makes damage swim when lighting changes. This validates a useful native proxy, not a unique physically recovered material. Strong specular glints are not required by the reference and should not be added merely to demonstrate light response.

## 95 acceptance meaning

A95 is a high subjective visual standard with approximately2–3point reviewer uncertainty, not a measured percent match. Score all seven categories independently. Require native geometry/material construction, passing camera-scale hierarchy, passing light-response proxy, and no material regression before claiming the representative panel system reaches95. Do not average away a weak category with stronger ones. A small diagnostic patch may receive local progress approval while the system remains below95. Only the user can approve a final lock.
