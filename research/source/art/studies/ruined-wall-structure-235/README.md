## Held V1 and construction-joint repair

Actual235 V1 showed nearly invisible courses and isolated little shelf-like ledges. `held-v1/` preserves the candidate. V2 is `scene-v2.blend`, with `course-v2-audit.json` and reusable `tools/ruined_wall_course_fix_235.py`.

The audit found most old horizontal joints already lay on the outer plane; a few vertical ties were recessed0.023–0.065m. Thus narrow grooves and weak interior ink were the principal readability problem, not a universal placement error. V2 anchors all cuts to the true plane, widens courses to0.052m and adds connected panel ties. It hides all seven isolated ledges and retains only two longer, edge-connected floor-layer fragments. All edited hosts and additions pass manifold checks. One Boolean needed a7mm vertical offset to avoid a coplanar overlap with existing damage. Parent separately strengthens interior ink and quiets the underlying cloud finish.
