# Scrap kit study 075

Native editable Blender study against DP-08, UP-01, and UP-03, with selected concept used for near/end pile composition. Baseline buildings, camera, and original scene remain intact.

## Integration

Append `075 Scrap integration` from `kit.blend` and link the collection to the destination scene. Objects already use world coordinates. Hide the exact prior rubble prefixes in `integration.json`. Do not add `KIT | 075 battered scrap masters` to the scene: it is a source library, not another placed pile. Ground and side-stone changes belong to the separate ground study.

The kit contains 12 folded sheet/channel variants with stamped ribs and hex fastener remnants, 7 torn hollow casing variants (3 with flanged mouths), and 5 battered closed housings. Near pile uses a shared version of the accepted service-metal finish; end pile adds restrained oxidized/mineral tones. Beveled rims use a physically reflective metal material. All faces, shells, folds, bolts, and rings are editable mesh geometry.

## Iteration history

Initial camera placement lost most of the pile below the frame. The second pass exposed too many upright crown-like torn rims. The third pass laid hollow objects sideways, replaced even generic noise with the accepted layered service finish, and added stamped ribs and bolt remnants. The fourth added larger anchor pieces, flanged pipe mouths, and physical rim highlights. Final placement turns and elevates three flanged mouths toward the camera to make circular forms legible among the plates.

This is a proposed composition and kit family, not user-approved final rubble. Further work can enrich unique machinery fragments and refine local contact/overlap after the pile's density and shape are reviewed.

## Final integration adjustment

After the standalone proofs, root critique requested a 40% smaller, lower, tilted center ring and 25% smaller, lower, tilted right ring; the large left ring stays. End hollow remnants now lie down. `kit.blend`, `scene.blend`, and the source generator contain these corrections. The standalone PNGs predate this last adjustment; use the root composite render to judge the final export.
