# Anime character study — 206

Airam and Miranda are separate full-body, rear-view anime treatments of the user-provided photographs. The originals remain private; these derived sprites are an explicitly authorized illustration/compositing experiment. They do not count toward native character-geometry acceptance.

The built-in imagegen tool produced one transparent PNG per person. Raw outputs are preserved separately from the selected PNGs; both selected files currently equal the raw output byte-for-byte. Prompts are saved alongside the images. No pixel-art version was derived from these assets.

Airam retains dark wavy shoulder-length hair, cream collage-print shirt, cuffed dark jeans and bare heels. Miranda retains brown-to-sage hair, ivory/red-heart camisole, dark trousers and the asymmetric barefoot rear pose. Generated fabric folds and fine graphic details are interpretations, not photographic reconstructions.

The lighting experiment uses separate upright alpha-textured cards at the existing player's depth, with assumed heights of1.72m and1.65m (Airam x=-0.30m, Miranda x=+0.55m, both y=-6.5m). A15%light-energy version tests that the cards actually respond. The full209scene is preserved on disk. The separate clean-background study excludes all15scale-placeholder meshes and eight proven character-local contact-ink strokes; genuine road ink remains.

Limits: these are flat cards. Their normals cannot recreate limb/hair self-shadow, true rim lighting, or full environmental occlusion. The isolated lighting pass does not claim a physically correct foot-contact shadow; a future simple proxy rig or small normal/depth asset would be needed. This is a bounded feasibility result, not a production character system.
