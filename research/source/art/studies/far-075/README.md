# Distant architecture study 075

Baseline: accepted alley scene 074. This study does not alter alley buildings, camera, landmark, lighting, or atmosphere. It hides only the non-rubble members of `013 Long ruined city - depth layers`. Collapsed rubble in that collection remains visible.

## Current direction

The user's follow-up favors **many tall, skinny faraway buildings**. The current candidate uses narrower footprints, shallow returns and more silhouettes across the existing seven distant depth bands. First attempts with broad apartment-like blocks were rejected; `render-v1.png`–`render-v3.png` preserve those experiments.

## Editable families

1. Raked slab: projecting angled foot, narrow vertical mass, roof receiver.
2. Service tower: clipped roof corner, offset crown, vertical piers and closed service risers.
3. Gallery tower: cantilevered upper face with an angled lower return, sparse horizontal window groups.

Window sliders and kick panels reduce to their primary shapes. Near distant bands retain receiver boxes, restrained pipe loops, broad corner pieces and a few side apertures. Tiny bolts, microcracks and high-frequency greebles are omitted. The nearest two material levels copy approved facade and service graphs; far levels use stepped normal-based native color shading. Existing volumetric depth remains unchanged.
