# 210 · Colosseum scale selection

Separate native variants of209, pending visual review and user selection. Canonical209 stays unchanged.

The target is consumption of the vertical gap above the highest visible Colosseum point:10%,20%,70%. It is not a10/20/70% direct enlargement. The measured scales are1.0487125,1.0975377,1.3429403. The highest baseline point is a masonry remnant at full-frame y375.7897 px; framing is measured separately.

All 3,531 collection objects plus the dedicated portable-kit companion `110 Landmark contact ink` (16,427 GP points) receive one coherent uniform transform through 860 hierarchy roots. Tunnels, inset platforms, wire,190 recessed wall damage and contact ink move together. The three earlier proofs with unscaled companion ink are held in `held-unscaled-contact-ink` and are not publishable. Mixed096/097 road and near-building GP owners remain untouched. The pivot uses the nearest horizontal surface point's XY and the landmark's lowest world Z. This fixes the nearest horizontal camera distance at191.3641488 m and ground elevation at−0.9630135 m. The exact nearest three-dimensional surface becomes0.041,0.084,0.345 m farther away as it rises. No geometry moves toward the camera. Both distance definitions are disclosed rather than conflated.

Sources remain native Blender scenes under baseline/10/20/70. The baseline preview is a documented downsample of the actual209 native4K render; only the three changed variants require new renders. Preview renders use3840×2885 at50% with the source global Freestyle width 2.666667 retained; Blender render-percentage applies its output-size factor automatically. The first thin-line10% attempt is held in held-thin-ink-10. Explicit149/156/161/192/205/207 guard callbacks run during background renders, including the separate ink architecture from192. The sun, sky, haze, street, camera, materials and all nonlandmark transforms remain fixed.

`tools/colosseum_scale_metrics_210.py` records full-resolution-equivalent projected geometry and distances. `tools/colosseum_scale_210.py -- build LABEL` builds one frozen variant from209 and verifies every evaluated landmark vertex follows the single transform, all nonlandmark world matrices remain unchanged, and actual highest-point visibility and target height. `-- render LABEL` renders only that frozen variant. LABEL isbaseline,10,20 or70. Coordinate GPU scheduling before rendering.

References: UCL-01, the project creator's original ChatGPT Images2.5 artwork, for landmark prominence; DP-05 by Darius Puia/BakaArts for architectural mass and negative space; HM-03 fromHeavy Metal(1981) for street depth. External references are study material, not game assets. No reference image is projected into the native scene.

The dedicated contact drawing is baked for the original view. After scaling, its visibility is recomputed against evaluated external opaque geometry, including render-visible collection-instance paths. Only hidden runs are removed; surviving native point and curve attributes remain exact. Sampling is at most 0.5 full-resolution pixels with midpoint checks and visibility-transition bisection. This does not regenerate newly exposed contacts that were absent from the original bake. Replay starts from the preserved unclipped scene or its retained original drawing, never from an already clipped result. The helper is independently reusable for new foreground buildings without applying any landmark scale.

Final gate: all three native previews and the matched four-way comparison were visually inspected. No old-position aperture contacts or external near-box/city contact overdraw remain. 10% and 20% keep the sun clear; 70% partially occludes it naturally. Native multilayer EXRs are retained for 10% and 20%. The optional 70% EXR export failed after the successful PNG save because its media-type setting was missing; that cache-only failure did not trigger another render. All versions remain selection studies, not a canonical replacement.
