# Alley damage distribution

Approved component vocabulary: concrete 045–046, sheet-metal specimen 047, chromatic runoff 048. Distribution 049 awaits user review.

Three levels of decisions:

1. Building identity: retain its established warm/cool finish and choose how much new weathering it receives. The side-road face stays quieter than exposed alley-facing panels.
2. Architectural region: bound a runoff field around a selected panel/window edge. Vary its horizontal extent, vertical reach, direction and strength. Face-normal gating suppresses undersides and perpendicular returns; it does not simulate occlusion by every ledge.
3. Individual loss: keep geometric damage sparse. The additional mirrored panel uses a 38 mm corner lift, compared with 85 mm on the first. The concrete support variants and service-specific corrosion remain separate.

The 049 source and audit record the regions. Field motifs derive from the approved runoff group; translation, reflection, scale and strength provide this first distribution. A larger scene would benefit from additional seeded motif families, rather than endlessly reusing these coordinates.

## Next visual family

Road and rubble need their own component-first treatment: fractured slabs with thickness, broken structural fragments, smaller angular debris, embedded grit, and coherent road cracks. Preserve the walkable lane and accepted distant-city scale. Do not try to solve those missing forms by adding more facade stains.
