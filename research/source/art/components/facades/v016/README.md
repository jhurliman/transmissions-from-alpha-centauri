# Section-driven buildings — v1

A building begins with a connected depth/height profile, extruded across its width. This master is 8.4 m wide and 20 m tall. Its local X follows the frontage; positive Y enters the building; Z is height. Place the collection instance without stretching finished details.

## Assembly order

1. Choose a connected profile: recessed base, projecting shoulder, upper wall, optional setback. Retain shared floor and platform heights.
2. Place structural columns and corbels beneath the projection. Use a restricted angle palette: 15° feet, 30° shoulder and 45° transitions in this study.
3. Close the mass with side returns following the same profile.
4. Assign a local surface frame to each plane. Fit broad panels and modeled openings in that frame.
5. Subdivide within structural bays, with a small number of distinct panel groups. Avoid equal detail everywhere.
6. Attach services to their actual receiving surfaces. Preserve approved existing routes when replacing a facade.

These angles and construction functions are authored interpretations. The selected artwork supplies visual evidence, not engineering drawings.
