Native editable masters in `architectural-extensions.blend`. Clean geometry only; unique damage remains a separate future layer. Used with the existing v014 facade and v002 service libraries.

| Master | Dimensions / rule | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| gangway_single_Y_8m | 8.24 m long, 1.61 m projection, 4.71 m high; one Y; arms 30° from vertical | Long skinny platform with one dramatic structural support |
| buttress_45 | 2.51 m high; concrete prism at 45° | Arrange by authored bay composition, not uniform intervals |
| column_recessed_4m8 | 4.76 m high, .66 m wide; recessed panel faces | Front-left building's two vertical panel columns |

Local X follows the facade; local Z is vertical; negative Y points into the alley. Wall attachment plane is Y=0. Instances remain unit scale. New dimensions should be built as parameterized variants, not stretched instances.

Building placement uses separate wall planes and real open intervals. The left service entrance and right side road are scene assemblies; their function is an interpretation of visible reference gaps. They are visual studies, not implemented game exits.

The long U-return reuses the service library's continuous hollow 180° elbow and straight sections, joined using saved bore connection frames. Service wall standoffs vary deliberately.

The left and right gangway variants shift the single Y support 1.6 m along the platform in opposite directions. Deck dimensions and member cross-sections remain unchanged.
