# Large service scale study

Native dimensioned masters used in alley review 029. All placed instances have unit scale.

- Primary: 1.8 m outside diameter, 4 m straight modules, 2.6 m maximum housing diameter.
- Companion: 0.6 m outside diameter, 3 m straight modules, 1.0 m housing diameter.
- Duct: 1.4 × 1.0 m section, 3 m modules.
- Primary sits at alley y=26 m; companion y=22.5 m; duct y=19 m.
- Primary terminates into a lower wall turn and upper projecting receiver.
- Companion turns into the wall at both ends.
- Duct terminates into a plinth and louver unit.

Local X follows the facade, Z is up and negative Y faces the alley. Wall-entry depth is dimensioned for the receiving scene and must be recalculated when the host changes. This is a scale proof; secondary detail and full integration with the general routing grammar remain future refinement.
