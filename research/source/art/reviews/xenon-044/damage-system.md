# Damage system: first specimen

## Provenance and scope
First right-side 45-degree support. Clean source remains available; the host uses a separate damaged collection. Native editable Boolean cutters produce the cavities and crack grooves. Exposed concrete uses its own rough aggregate material. No image projection or damage decal.

## Controls to carry forward
- Deterministic seed, severity, affected region, branch count and crack taper.
- Separate coating failure, substrate loss, fractures and deposits. Do not reuse one noise mask for every process.
- Protect connection regions and preserve the intended silhouette until an authored collapse pass is requested.
- Judge major losses at the game camera and fracture structure in closeup. Avoid enlarging all hairline detail to force visibility.
- Keep live cutters for iteration; bake a duplicate for export and validate connectedness, normals and triangle cost before production.

## Next proof points
- An edge spall that wraps a corner rather than repeating on only one planar face.
- A separate runoff mask originating under a joint or opening, following gravity and surface orientation.
- Metal-specific abrasion/corrosion, independent of concrete cracking and architectural paint masks.
- A low/medium/high damage comparison using the same seed and component, including zero damage recovering the clean master.

This is visual construction, not a stress analysis or a fracture simulation.
